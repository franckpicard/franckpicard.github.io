## Research

My work lies at the interface of **statistics, machine learning and molecular biology**. I develop methodology for high-dimensional and structured biological data, with a current emphasis on single-cell and spatial omics. A central objective is to combine flexible statistical learning with **rigorous inference, statistical risk control and interpretability**.

<div class="card-grid">
<div class="scai-card"><h3>Statistical inference</h3><p>Hypothesis testing, kernel methods and statistical learning for high-dimensional biological data, with an emphasis on <strong>risk control and reproducibility</strong>.</p></div>
<div class="scai-card"><h3>Spatial single-cell data</h3><p>Spatial statistics and point processes to characterize tissue organization and molecular spatial variability.</p></div>
<div class="scai-card"><h3>Explainable AI</h3><p>Sensitivity analysis and interpretable learning to identify the cells and molecular features driving biological differences.</p></div>
<div class="scai-card"><h3>Representation & integration</h3><p>Machine learning and statistical methods for the representation and integration of high-dimensional, heterogeneous and multi-omic single-cell data.</p></div>

## Methods driven by biological questions

I develop these methods in close collaboration with experimental and computational biologists, particularly in **developmental and cancer biology**. These collaborations confront new statistical and AI methodologies with challenging biological questions and real single-cell and spatial datasets.

## SCAI

I lead the **SCAI — Artificial Intelligence for Single-Cell Data Analysis** team at the [Laboratory of Biology and Modeling of the Cell (LBMC)](https://www.ens-lyon.fr/LBMC) in Lyon. The team develops statistical learning and AI methodologies for single-cell and spatial omics in close interaction with molecular and cell biologists.
