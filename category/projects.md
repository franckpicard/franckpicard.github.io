My current projects connect methodological developments in statistics and AI with challenging questions arising from single-cell and spatial biology, often through collaborative research programs.

## AI4scMed

**Artificial Intelligence for Single-Cell Based Medicine**  
A PEPR Santé Numérique project focused on methodological foundations for single-cell based medicine. The project develops statistical and AI methods for robust inference, integration and interpretation of single-cell data.

[Visit the AI4scMed website](https://ai4scmed.github.io)

## SpaceTranscriptomix

I develop statistical methodology for **spatial transcriptomics and spatially resolved single-cell data**, combining kernel methods, point processes and spatial statistics to characterize tissue organization and molecular variability with application to Pituitary tumors (with P. Bertolino, CRCL).

## SingleStatOmics — previous project

The ANR **SingleStatOmics** project developed statistical learning approaches for single-cell data, including probabilistic models, dimension reduction, optimal transport and integration methods.

[Visit the SingleStatOmics website](http://anr-singlestatomics.pages.math.cnrs.fr/)
