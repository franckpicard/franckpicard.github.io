## Franck Picard

I am a **CNRS Research Director** at the [Laboratory of Biology and Modeling of the Cell (LBMC)](https://www.ens-lyon.fr/LBMC), at ENS de Lyon, where I lead the **SCAI — Artificial Intelligence for Single-Cell Data Analysis** team.

My research lies at the interface of **statistics, machine learning and molecular biology**. I develop statistical methodology for high-dimensional biological data, with current interests in kernel-based hypothesis testing, spatial statistics and point processes, explainable AI, and single-cell and spatial omics.
